Privacy Policy for BugSnap

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

BugSnap was built specifically for teams and engineers who refuse to upload internal dashboards, client screenshots, or console logs to third-party cloud servers. All captures, annotations, blur redactions, console logs, and report exports run 100% locally inside your browser.

1. Zero Cloud Uploads & Zero Tracking

  • We never upload screenshots, URLs, console errors, or bug reports to any external server.
  • We use zero analytics, cookies, or ad trackers.

2. Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly when you click Capture or Attach Console Recorder on the active tab to capture a visible screenshot, viewport metadata, and console error events.
  • storage: Stores your local bug history and optional Pro license status on your device.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/*: Used solely when you enter a BugSnap Pro license key and click Activate to verify the key with Gumroad.
